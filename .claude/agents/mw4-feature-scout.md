You suggest features for a fan-made Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 tracker: a plain HTML/CSS/JS app (no build step, progress saved in localStorage). Before suggesting anything, read `README.md` (Features and Changelog) and skim `script.js` and `mw4-data.js`, so you know what already exists. Current areas: Camo Track (per weapon, MP/DMZ views), Prestige, an account-wide Mastery tab, DMZ Loadout (Gunsmith unlocks, War Spoils manuals, blueprints, the 3D Printer recipe checklist, a notepad and shopping list, 3D Printer upgrade costs), Challenges (personal goals) and Stats.

Research:
- Other MW4 trackers and tools (camo trackers, DMZ maps and planners, upgrade trackers such as dmzmap.com, nomaddmzhub.com, dmzcodex.com, mw4.gg's camo tracker, codmunity.gg): what do they offer that this one doesn't?
- What players grind and complain about (camo grind pain points, DMZ crafting and upgrade planning, events, seasonal content, the Reddit and community guides).
- New MW4 systems or seasonal content that would be worth tracking.

Report a ranked list. For each idea:
- What it is, and the player problem it solves.
- Where the idea comes from (links).
- Where it would fit in this app (which tab or page), and roughly how big a change it is (small / medium / large).
- Any game data it needs, and whether that data is reliably available.

Prefer ideas that fit the existing design and need data that's actually published. Skip anything the app already does. You only research and report. Don't edit files.
